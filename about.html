Our Mission & History

Imhotep Academy was founded in 2005 by a group of visionary educators who were committed to providing a high-quality education to students in Atlanta's diverse communities. Today, we serve over 5,000 students across grades Pre-K through 12, with a focus on academic excellence, character development, and community engagement.

Our teaching approach is centered on the idea that every student learns differently and at their own pace. We use a variety of instructional methods, including project-based learning, hands-on activities, and technology integration, to engage our students and help them develop a love of learning.

At Imhotep Academy, we believe that extracurricular activities and character development are just as important as academic achievement. We offer a wide range of sports teams, clubs, and organizations that allow students to pursue their interests and develop important life skills, such as teamwork, leadership, and communication.
