Our Mission & History

Imhotep Academy has a rich history dating back to 1922, when it was founded by a group of visionary educators who sought to provide a high-quality education to students from diverse backgrounds. Today, our school continues to thrive as a premier private school in Atlanta, offering a comprehensive curriculum that prepares students for success in college and beyond.

At Imhotep Academy, we believe that every child learns differently and that our teachers should be flexible in their approach. Our teaching approach is centered around the needs of each individual student, with a focus on project-based learning, hands-on activities, and real-world applications.

From sports teams to music programs, art clubs to drama productions, Imhotep Academy offers a wide range of extracurricular activities that cater to diverse interests and talents. Our students are encouraged to explore their passions and develop their unique strengths, whether on or off the field.
